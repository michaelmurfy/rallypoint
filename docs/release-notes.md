## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.7

**Red Alert 2 fixes macOS windowed sizing and pointer scaling, sharpens the loading screen and HUD text, restores the score-screen cursor, and adds 2x anti-aliasing for units and buildings.** Generals is untouched.

- **Windowed mode and macOS screen sizes.** Turning on **Windowed** with **Screen size** now fills your usable desktop instead of opening a small 1280x720 window, picking a window size in Settings automatically switches Windowed on, and Retina displays map every resolution choice to the right window size.
- **Loading screen and HUD text.** The skirmish loading screen now shows the map's real name (such as **Malibu Cliffs (2-6)** instead of `xmp17du.map`) and draws the loading text, sidebar credits, tooltips and in-game messages with the TrueType font at native screen pixels.
- **Right-sized mouse cursor and score screen pointer.** The in-game pointer is scaled for Retina and high-DPI screens instead of looking oversized on macOS, and the system cursor comes back on the skirmish score screen and in-game popups.
- **Anti-aliasing and cleaner voxel units.** Voxel tanks, aircraft and ships fill in 1-pixel projection pinholes on slopes and turns, and units, buildings, cameos and cursors run through a 2x edge-smoothing pass.

## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.6

Red Alert 2 drew the battlefield at native pixels with a separate interface scale, switched menu text to TrueType, added the Yuri's Revenge superweapon targeting cursors and official country flags from your game archives, kept healthy buildings on their undamaged frames, straightened unit movement, and gave the Psychic Dominator its permanent capture.

## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.5

Red Alert 2 got a 2x sidebar on high-resolution screens, right-click minimap orders, the Psychic Tower, Cloning Vats, Genetic Mutator, Psychic Dominator and Psychic Reveal, spiralling plane crashes, healthier fog and wall pathing, a resign score screen, and a cleaner launcher Play and Maps layout.
