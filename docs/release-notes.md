## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.9

**Red Alert 2's Play page is now laid out like Generals', and both games' skirmish setup opens with a bit of flair.** Plus a handful of in-game fixes for Red Alert 2.

- **A tidier Play page for Red Alert 2.** One header button takes you to the full skirmish setup under Maps. Quick Skirmish keeps opponents, difficulty and its start button. The options that were already under Maps are gone from here.
- **Screen options on the Play page.** Window size, windowed, interface size and world zoom now sit under Options, like Generals, instead of in a separate Settings tab.
- **Skirmish setup grows out of its button.** Click Skirmish under Maps (either game) and the setup grows out of the button, then shrinks back into it when you close it. Reduced motion skips the animation.
- **The camera stops at the map edge.** No more half a screen of black shroud past the border.
- **Smoother shroud along the edge.** The map border gets the same soft fog edge as the rest of the map.
- **Music starts somewhere new.** Every launch used to open on the same track. Now the first theme is random.
- **The score screen has music.** It plays the game's own score theme.
- **Skirmish icon stays put.** The play icon no longer shrinks away in the Red Alert 2 map list.
