## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.10

**Yuri looks like Yuri again, and the camera really does stop at the map edge this time.** Red Alert 2 only. Generals is untouched.

- **Yuri's own sidebar.** Playing Yuri used to give you the Soviet sidebar. You now get his purple palette and radar logo, read from your Yuri's Revenge files the way the original game does it.
- **Yuri's loading screen in colour.** It was drawn with the wrong palette and came out grey. It now uses the game's own Yuri palette.
- **The camera stops at the map edge.** The last fix let you scroll until the whole map was off screen. The view now stays on the map, and skirmish maps keep to their playable area like the original.
- **The WARNING gauge moves.** The little monitor above the menu buttons only animated on the main menu. It now runs on every menu page.
- **Menu buttons press properly.** The label no longer jumps down when you click a button. Only the button itself shows the press.

## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.9

Red Alert 2's Play page now follows the Generals layout, with screen options under Options, the skirmish setup grows out of its button in both games, and Red Alert 2 got a softer shroud edge, a random first music track and the score theme on the score screen.
