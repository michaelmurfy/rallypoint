## Rallypoint 0.8.2

**Computer players that march properly, and a launcher with some colour in it.** Attacks arrive as a column instead of a traffic jam, and Rallypoint itself got a coat of paint.

- **No more walking backwards.** A computer player's team used to start an attack by heading for the nearest point on its route, even one behind it. It now sets off forwards.
- **No more queues at waypoints.** Units used to line up for one exact spot at every turn of the route and shove each other around it. They now pass by and keep moving.
- **Groups stay together.** Units keep their place near the route, and never aim for a spot in a cliff, in water or inside a building.
- **Stuck units keep their orders.** In 0.8.1 a stuck unit on an attack route was sent to the next point and then forgot the rest. It now carries on along the route.
- **Fighting is not being stuck.** In 0.8.1 a unit that stopped to shoot for 8 seconds could lose its order. Not any more.
- **Not compatible with 0.8.1 in multiplayer.** Computer players move differently, so everyone in a room needs 0.8.2.
- **A new look.** Every section has its own colour, mods get coloured tiles, panels and buttons have more depth, and the Rallypoint badge finally has room to breathe. Nothing has moved.

## Rallypoint 0.8.1

Computer players' units that get boxed in are helped out (a new path, then a nudge, then their order is dropped), and the Stronger AI setting follows the room online while skirmish keeps its own level.

## Rallypoint 0.8.0

A Stronger AI setting that works in every mod and is set per room by its master, eight community fixes to computer players and pathfinding, mods' own general names in the skirmish setup, and Control Bar Pro fixes for Shockwave.
