## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.8

**Red Alert 2 gets its own title art on the Play page, a prominent skirmish button, and the RA2 side now matches Generals' Play layout.** Generals is untouched.

- **Game art in the Play header.** Red Alert 2 now reads its own title art (`launchermd.bmp` / `LAUNCHER.BMP` from the player's game folder — the Yuri's Revenge / Red Alert 2 logos) and shows it in the Play page hero, like Generals' `Install_Final.bmp`.
- **Skirmish play button restored.** A prominent "Start skirmish" button now sits in the Play hero (in addition to the Quick Skirmish card's button), so the primary play action is always visible without scrolling.

## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.7

Red Alert 2 fixed macOS windowed sizing and pointer scaling, sharpened the loading screen and HUD text, restored the score-screen cursor, and added 2x anti-aliasing for units and buildings.

## Rallypoint 0.10.0-beta.6

Red Alert 2 drew the battlefield at native pixels with a separate interface scale, switched menu text to TrueType, added the Yuri's Revenge superweapon targeting cursors and official country flags from your game archives, kept healthy buildings on their undamaged frames, straightened unit movement, and gave the Psychic Dominator its permanent capture.
